Course Description   This course introduces the Internet of Things (IoT) ecosystem, architecture, and applications. Topics include: IoT predecessors (WSN, M2M, CPS) and emergence; IoT sensing, actuation, and edge/cloud processing topologies; connectivity and communication protocols (IEEE 802.15.4, Zigbee, LoRa, NB-IoT, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, MQTT, CoAP); IoT interoperability standards and fog computing; and emerging paradigms and case studies across smart agriculture, vehicular IoT, healthcare, and smart cities. Each topic comes with slides, worksheets, study notes and illustrated comics.

Course Information

Prerequisites
Basic programming, computer networks, and fundamentals of electronics/sensors.
